# FC-00072 — Executive PR

**Date:** 2026-11-11 · **Slice:** M · **Risk:** Medium · **Status:** done · **Affected entities:** All

## What
1. A new pay method, **Executive PR**: a fixed weekly amount the accountant pays by deposit. It is always Flat (choosing it on the Payroll tab makes the row Flat, and choosing another method afterwards makes it Hourly again; a settings-file row with this method loads as Flat, amount from Flat Amount or 0; adding someone with it creates them Flat).
2. On the payroll report (combined and per entity; Excel, PDF, preview and the editable copy) each entity's Executive PR people print in their own block **after the entity's subtotal**: a label row "Executive PR — <entity>", their rows (Method "Executive PR", Actual Total and Deposit = the weekly amount, no hours or rate), and "<entity> Executive PR subtotal".
3. **GRAND TOTAL stays the hourly payroll.** After it: **EXECUTIVE PR TOTAL** (sum of the Executive PR subtotals) and **TOTAL WITH EXECUTIVE PR** (both together; its Deposit is every deposit the accountant pays). All are formulas.
4. Stats and Labor Cost (sheets and screen) leave Executive PR out: it is not hourly labor.
5. Sorting puts Executive PR after Contract Check. Week over week and the settings file name it "Executive PR".

## Why
The owner, 2026-11-11, on the fixed-pay people on the accountant's file: "They go in a separate section under their respective entities titled "Executive PR"" and "I want all of these people to show up on our final exports for each week".

## Where in UI
Payroll Calculation (Pay Method buttons), new-employee dialog, payroll report.

## Touches
display, exports, settings, roster

## Risk
Medium: new people on the report; the existing totals (GRAND TOTAL) do not change.

## Reversibility
Fully reversible via one revert commit. Settings files written with "Executive PR" would read as an unknown method in the old tool (the person keeps Deposit).

## Definition of Done
1. An Executive PR person appears with no punches, Flat, Deposit = weekly amount.
2. The block, its subtotal and the two total rows appear in Excel, PDF and preview; formulas agree.
3. GRAND TOTAL unchanged by Executive PR; Stats and Labor Cost leave them out.

## Out of scope
- Monthly bonuses (undecided; next card).
- Taxes or net amounts.

## Assumptions
- Executive PR totals are kept apart from the GRAND TOTAL so validated weekly totals keep their meaning.

## Open questions
None.

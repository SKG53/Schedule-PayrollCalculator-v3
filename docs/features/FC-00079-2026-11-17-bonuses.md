# FC-00079 — Bonuses: weekly, monthly and specialty, each paid the way you choose

**Date:** 2026-11-17 · **Slice:** M · **Risk:** Medium · **Status:** done · **Affected entities:** All
**Depends on:** FC-00078 (bonuses belong to a person's entity assignment)

## What
1. A person can have any number of bonuses in a run. Each bonus has:
   - a **type**: Weekly, Monthly or Specialty;
   - an **amount**;
   - its own **pays by** choice: Deposit, Cash or Contract Check;
   - an optional note.
2. **Only a Weekly bonus with "Recurring" ticked carries over:** it appears again next week, already ticked, until changed or removed.
3. **Everything else belongs only to the week it was entered:** Monthly, Specialty, and an unticked Weekly. They do not appear the next week.
4. **Entered on the Payroll tab:** a Bonus cell on each row opens a small editor to add, change or remove a bonus.
5. **Pays-by default:** Deposit for Deposit and Deposit + Cash people, Cash for Cash people, Contract Check for Contract Check people. It can be changed per bonus.
6. **Money:**
   - A bonus adds to the person's Total and to the column it pays by.
   - A cash bonus joins the cash amount before it is rounded to whole dollars.
   - A deposit bonus for a Deposit + Cash person adds to the deposit on top of their set deposit amount; their cash stays hours × rate minus the set deposit.
7. **Saved:** recurring Weekly bonuses with the assignment; one-off bonuses in the register's This Week sheet. Every change is one History line and undoable; the snapshot carries them.
8. **Shown:** a Bonus column on the payroll report (FC-00080) and on the accountant file (FC-00081, deposit bonuses only).

## Why
The owner, 2026-11-16: "I need a bunch of different ways to put the bonus amount down. one way is weekly, another way is monthly, another way is specialty… it will be set in the tool, and it can be saved when I export the settings for that week." "[Specialty] Should not auto show up the following week. a monthly bonus should also not auto check the next week. only a weekly bonus shows up week after week automatically. with the box checked that it is a recurring weekly bonus." "the bonus needs its own toggle for deposit, cash, contract check."

## Where in UI
The Payroll tab (a Bonus cell and editor per row) and the Employee Register (recurring bonuses on the assignment).

## Touches
pay calculation, payroll tab, register, reports

## Risk
Medium: it changes Total, Deposit, Cash and Contract Check whenever a bonus exists. With no bonuses, pay is unchanged on every real week.

## Reversibility
One revert commit. Bonuses saved in a register are ignored by older versions.

## Definition of Done
1. A Weekly + Recurring bonus is in next week's run after the register is saved and loaded. Monthly, Specialty and an unticked Weekly are not.
2. Each pays-by choice moves the amount into exactly that column and into the Total.
3. A cash bonus rounds together with the person's cash, and a Deposit + Cash person's deposit bonus is added to the deposit. Checked by hand on test cases.
4. The report's Excel formulas include the bonus and agree with the tool.
5. No bonuses: pay unchanged on all real weeks.

## Out of scope
- Bonus rules worked out by the tool (targets, percentages).
- Paying a bonus for a week other than the run it is entered in.

## Assumptions
- The pays-by default follows the person's pay method as in 5.
- A bonus needs no hours and does not count toward the 40 hours for overtime.

None.
